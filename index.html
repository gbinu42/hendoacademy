About Us

Hendo Academy is dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage of the Nasranies of India, also known as Saint Thomas Christians. We focus on the study of Syriac language, the historical foundations of the Syro Malabar Church, and the unique traditions of the Nasrani community.

Our mission is to advance scholarly research, education, and awareness about the ancient Christian community in Kerala and its connections to the broader Syriac Christian world.
