Tharisappally Syrian Christian Copper Plates: Karshon Transcript

July 4, 2025 Josekutty Abraham, Binu George, Lulu Mathews Syriac, Malayalam, Copper Plates, Karshon

The Tharisappally Syrian Christian Copper Plates are among the most significant historical documents pertaining to the St. Thomas Christians of Kerala. These copper plates, dating back to the 9th century, contain royal grants and privileges given to the Syrian Christian community.

This publication presents a transcription of the Tharisappally Copper Plates from manuscript MS Syriaque 186, housed at the Bibliothèque nationale de France. The original plates, dating to AD 849, were translated in AD 1601 by Ittimani Kathanar under the direction of Bishop Francis Roz, rendering the Vattezhuth inscriptions into Karshon Malayalam. This digital transcription was carefully prepared in 2024 by Josekutty Abraham, Binu George, and Lulu Mathews of Hendo Academy, providing renewed access to this significant historical document.

You can also visit hudra.day to see higher quality images of the manuscript.
